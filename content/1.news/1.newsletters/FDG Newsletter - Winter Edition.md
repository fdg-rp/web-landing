## **Development Highlights**

The development team has once again been extremely busy over the past few months.

The developers noted on each update are not conclusive, a significant amount of time goes into each project and most require the ' insight of multiple members of our team and their experience to complete these massive projects. A massive thank you to all the developers for the work they do.

### **🚛 Trucking Overhaul \- IGWolf 🚛**

![TRUCK](https://imgur.com/glBa9rG.png)

One of the largest job updates this season was a major expansion to the trucking system.

Trucking, now named “Quack Logistics” has now expanded well beyond just driving trucks with experienced truckers gaining access to entirely new forms of missions.

By completing regular trucking jobs, players are able to earn levels with higher levels providing these new missions:

* **Maritime Freight** at Level 80  
* **Aviation Freight** at Level 100  
* Cargo, gold and illegal freight contracts  
* New heavy-haul trucks  
* Logistics Cache lootboxes  
* Expanded illegal shipment rewards  
* Improved level-based loot  
* A refreshed trucking tablet and mission interface

Maritime missions allow experienced players to purchase and operate the **Marine Tug**, while players reaching the highest levels of trucking can eventually purchase the **Cargo Skylift** and take freight into the air.

The update has transformed trucking into a much larger long-term progression system and gives dedicated truckers something substantial to continue working towards.

Massive shoutout to **IGWolf** and the development team for the work put into this.

### **💰 [Smash & Grab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J7dBDCFnT7Q) 💰**

![SMASH and grab](https://imgur.com/jRzstJL.png)

One of the biggest completely new activities released this season was **Smash & Grab**, introduced by the development team.

Initially released as an exclusive early-access activity for Fat Duck Family members (another amazing perk for supporting the server), Smash & Grab later opened to the entire server.

The activity introduces a crew-based criminal progression system built around five different tiers of robbery:

* Convenience Stores  
* General Stores  
* Retail Stores  
* Luxury Stores  
* Fleeca Banks

Each robbery contains a variety of hands-on tasks including holding up clerks, cracking safes, hacking computers, drilling deposit boxes, looting registers and dealing with alarm systems.

Crews earn XP as they complete jobs, eventually unlocking more difficult locations with greater payouts and access to rarer loot.

There is also a strong risk to reward element. Crews can push further into a robbery and complete more tasks for greater rewards, but doing so gives law enforcement more time to respond.

Along with all other tasks Smash & Grab also has its own lootbox which can be gathered from robbing stores.

Huge shoutout to the development team for the work involved in bringing the activity into the server and continuing to improve it following release.

### **🏎️ Widebodies & Lootboxes \- Vehicle Team 🏎️**

Vehicle progression also received a substantial shake-up this season with the introduction of a completely different approach to **Widebody builds and lootboxes**

The previous Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 widebody system has been removed.

Widebody upgrades now use their own recipes, meaning lower-tier vehicles are easier to build while highly desirable performance vehicles require significantly more parts and progression.

Lootboxes have also become far more integrated throughout the server and can now be obtained through activities including:

* Drug Sales  
* Break & Enter  
* Fishing  
* Smash & Grab  
* Hunting  
* Trucking  
* Scavenging  
* Chop Shop  
* Car Parts  
* Racing

Different activities can provide different components required for vehicle builds, giving players a reason to participate in more than one gameplay loop rather than endlessly grinding the same activity.

An absolutely massive shoutout to the vehicle team for this one.

### **🎁 Skill Rewards \- Martibo 🎁**

![martibo clothes](https://imgur.com/k44hDmD.png)

An example of custom clothing^

Another major progression update came from **Martibo** with the introduction of **Skill Rewards**.

Previously, reaching the level cap of a skill effectively meant reaching the end of its progression. After this change that is no longer the case, players who continue levelling after maxing a skill can now earn **Skill Points** specific to that activity. Taking inspiration from a popular cozy grinder “Palia” for this one.

Each skill has its own reward dealer located around the world where these points can be redeemed. Many of these rewards are unique and cannot simply be purchased elsewhere.

Massive shoutout to **Martibo** for adding another layer of progression for people who wish to pursue it.

## **What's Been Going On?**

### **🦆 FDG Portal & Digital Licences \- Deveraux 🦆**

![deveraux](https://imgur.com/736bgeH.png)

Fat Duck Family has continued receiving new features throughout the season, with **Deveraux** introducing and expanding the **FDG Portal**.

The Portal gives players a central location to view their achievements, check outstanding support tickets and claim special FDF rewards.

New rewards have continued rotating through the Portal during the season, including claimable XP boosts and other benefits.

FDF members also received **Digital Licences** through the Government application on their phone.

The licence system integrates with achievements and allows players to unlock different stickers and titles based on what they have achieved in-game.

Huge shoutout to **Deveraux** for continuing to build out additional features and rewards for FDF supporters.

### **👑 Cyber Bar & King of the Hill \- Golden-Eagle 👑**

![cyberbar](https://imgur.com/HtsZ6wX.png)

**Golden-Eagle** and Cyber Bar have also been busy this season with the introduction and continued testing of **King of the Hill**.

KOTH is a team-based game mode where groups compete to maintain control of a designated area for as long as possible.

Players earn points by holding the objective, with the first team reaching the target score taking the round.

The mode includes its own progression system through **levels and Cyber Bux**, allowing participants to gain access to weapons and vehicles as they progress.

Cyber Bar has also undergone further upgrades and launched a **server-wide 3v3 tournament**, giving players another competitive activity outside of ordinary roleplay.

Shoutout to **Golden-Eagle** and everyone involved with Cyber Bar for continuing to provide different events and activities for the community.

### **🏆 Community Competitions \- BaileyG & Community Team 🏆**

Community competitions have continued throughout Winter, giving players opportunities to get creative and have their work recognised.

One of the biggest this season was the **Stunt Jump Competition**, hosted by **BaileyG**.

Players were given almost complete freedom over their submissions, whether that meant launching themselves in a car, bike, bicycle or somehow attempting it on foot.

Following submissions and voting, the winners were:

**1st Place:** *This Close to Greatness* by **midday**

**2nd Place:** *The Great Escape* by **amar**

**3rd Place:** *Tiel Shot* by **Tiel**

Congratulations again to all three winners and everyone who submitted an entry.

The recent **Parachute Competition** has also had its winning designs implemented directly into the server, with **Tay** getting the textures working through parachute customisation.

Community competitions remain one of the best ways for players to get their own creativity permanently represented within FDG.

Massive shoutout to **BaileyG**, **Tay** and everyone else involved in organising, judging and implementing community competitions.

### **🔧 Business Executive Opportunities 🔧**

The Board of Directors also opened Expressions of Interest for executive teams across all mechanic businesses.

The tenure system gives players the opportunity to submit a complete executive team and take responsibility for running one of the city's major mechanic businesses.

Executives are expected to actively manage staff, maintain customer service, develop new ideas and organise events or promotions that create further community interaction.

It gives players looking for more responsibility within the business scene an opportunity to directly influence how a business operates.

### **🏁 Racing Season 5 \- Martibo 🏁**

![racing](https://imgur.com/YZ3Qt6v.png)

\*photo credit to Martibo\*

The racing scene has continued moving into another season under **Martibo**.

Season 5 development has included random lootbox drops after races, Double XP Tokens, new calling cards, updated tracks and a range of smaller quality-of-life improvements.

Massive shoutout to **Martibo** for continuing to support and develop the racing community heading into another season.
